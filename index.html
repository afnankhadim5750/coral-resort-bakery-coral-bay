About the bakery

The village bakery

Coral Resort Bakery sits in the Coral Bay village centre, an easy walk from anywhere in town. It is the kind of shop travellers plan a stop around: a great range of bakery items, friendly staff, and a busy counter that says a lot about a place.

Visitors come for the pies and pastries, stay for the coffee, and leave with a box for the road. The shop offers outdoor seating, takeout, and options for vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free diets.

  • Outdoor seating and takeout
  • Parking available, wheelchair accessible
  • Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options

Coral Bay

Breakfast before the reef

Coral Bay is a small town with a big reef on its doorstep, and the bakery is part of the morning routine: grab a pie and a coffee, then head for the water.

The shop is right in the village centre, within easy walking distance of anywhere in Coral Bay, with parking available and outdoor seating for a slow start.
